How it works

Proof from the moment of capture to the moment of viewing

Three simple promises — kept from the camera to the person watching.

01 — CAPTURE

Proof at the moment of recording

Proof is created the instant a photo or video is taken — on the device, as it happens. It can't be bolted onto a file after the fact, which is exactly what makes it worth trusting.

02 — SURVIVE

Proof that travels

Files get compressed, re-encoded, and re-posted as they move through messaging apps and social platforms. Reality's proof doesn't ride inside the file — so sharing can't strip it away.

03 — VERIFY

Free for anyone to check

Drop a file on the verify page and get a clear, evidence-based answer in seconds. Verification is free and public — always. No account, no paywall.

Verdicts

We say exactly what we can prove — and no more

Binary real-or-fake stamps break trust the first time they're wrong. Reality grades every verdict honestly.

Verified capture

This file matches a recording whose proof was captured at the moment it was made. The strongest claim we make — and we can back it.

Registered

We've seen this content before and know when it entered the record, but we can't prove how it was originally captured. A weaker claim, clearly labeled as one.

No record — not "fake"

Content we've never seen is simply unknown. Absence of proof is not proof of fakery, and we will never pretend otherwise.

Why now

Detection is a losing race. Proof isn't.

Detectors age badly

Every tool that spots today's AI fakes becomes training material for tomorrow's. Betting on detection means betting that fakes stop improving. They won't. Proof captured at the source doesn't decay as generators get better.

Labels get stripped in transit

Approaches that attach credentials to the file itself lose them the moment a platform re-encodes an upload. Reality was built for how media actually moves: shared, compressed, screenshotted, re-posted.

"You can't decide real-or-fake by staring at a finished file. But a camera that was present when the light hit the sensor can vouch for what it saw." — the idea behind Reality
