## [intro]
Most people can't tell AI from real by looking. Here is what people look for, why it no longer settles anything, and how to prove a photo is real instead of guessing.

## [text] What people look for
These are the usual tells. They are worth a look.

- **Hands and fingers.** Extra, missing or merged fingers, or hands that bend the wrong way.
- **Text and signs.** Lettering that turns to nonsense on shop signs, number plates and labels.
- **Reflections and shadows.** Mirrors, windows and shadows that don't agree with the rest of the scene.
- **Repeated textures.** Brickwork, leaves, fabric or crowds that repeat in a pattern that is too regular.
- **Too-clean backgrounds.** Surfaces with no dust, wear or clutter, and edges that melt into the background.

But they are not proof. Current generators pass most of them, and a careful AI edit to a real photo shows none of them at all. Tools that score an image as "likely AI" give you a guess, not proof, and they can be wrong in both directions.

## [text] The other way: proof instead of guessing
Instead of asking "does this look real?", ask "can this be proven real?"

An AI image has no record, so it traces back to nothing. A real photo that was later edited with AI no longer matches its record. Either way, it can't be proven real.

## [faq] Questions
### [ai-tell] How can I tell if a photo is AI?
Often you can't, by eye or with a tool. In a 2025 test, people called AI images right 52% of the time and real photos 49%, about a coin toss ([Conjointly](https://conjointly.com/blog/real-vs-ai-images-2025/)). The dependable answer comes from the other direction: ask for a photo that can be proven real.

### [ai-check] Can RIALITI tell whether an image is AI?
No. It proves real ones. A photo taken in the app can be shown to be real and unedited. Anything without a record, AI or not, can't be proven either way.

### Does it work on screenshots?
It works on photos taken in RIALITI Capture and on copies of those photos sent on, for example through WhatsApp. A screenshot of some other image, or a photo from another camera app, has no record to check.

### Does compression break it?
No. Sending a photo through WhatsApp makes it smaller, and the copy can still be checked. Editing the photo is what breaks the match.
